The Craftsman

Passion
Carved into Every Grain

"I don't build furniture. I build heirlooms — pieces that carry the memory of the hands that made them long after those hands are gone."

Growing up surrounded by timber yards and the smell of freshly cut wood, carpentry was never a career choice — it was an inheritance. With over twelve years of dedicated practice across Gold Coast and beyond, I've developed a philosophy that places material, honesty, and longevity above trend or convenience.

Every commission begins with a conversation: about your space, your life, the way you want to feel when you walk into a room. From that conversation, and from the unique character of the timber selected, something singular is created — built by hand, from sustainably sourced materials, with joinery techniques that will outlast the century.

Ultimate Builds is not a production line. It is a studio, a workshop, and a promise: that what you receive will be the finest version of what you imagined.

Jordan Clarke

Master Carpenter & Founder

How We Work

From First Word
to Finished Piece

01

Discovery & Conversation

Every project begins with listening. We explore your vision, your space, your lifestyle, and the story you want your piece to tell. No two conversations are the same.

02

Design & Material Selection

Detailed drawings and timber selection together. You'll handle the actual planks being considered — seeing the grain, the figure, the character of the wood before a single cut is made.

03

Handcraft & Build

The work happens in the workshop, by hand, with traditional and modern joinery techniques combined. You receive progress updates and are welcome to visit during this stage.

04

Delivery & Installation

White-glove delivery and installation by the same hands that built the piece. We don't leave until everything is perfect — and we don't consider a project finished until you do.
