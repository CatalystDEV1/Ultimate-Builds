Dedication
Carved into Every Job

"I don't cut corners — I build them. Every single job I take on gets the same care and attention, whether it's a deck, a frame, or a full renovation."

Growing up on the Central Coast, Lochie McClelland developed a love for working with his hands from an early age. After completing his carpentry apprenticeship in 2025, he launched Ultimate Builds as a sole trader with a simple philosophy: deliver work so good that clients never need to call anyone else.

In the short time since going out on his own, Lochie has already built a reputation for reliability, clean workmanship, and genuine care for every project he touches. From structural framing and precision flooring installations to outdoor decking and bathroom renovations, he brings a level of finish that punches well above his years.

Operating across Terrigal, Avoca Beach, Kincumber, Erina, Wamberal, and the wider Central Coast, Lochie keeps his client list intentionally manageable — so that every project receives the focused attention it deserves.

How We Work

From First Call
to Finished Build

Your Free Quote
